STARTUP COMMANDS
----------------
  pip install -r requirements.txt
  python seed_data.py
  uvicorn main:app --reload

  Open browser: http://localhost:8000

NOTE: MongoDB must be running on localhost:27017 before starting.
  Windows:  net start MongoDB
  macOS:    brew services start mongodb/brew/mongodb-community
  Linux:    sudo systemctl start mongod

================================================================================
                   SUGGESTED DEMO WALKTHROUGH (5 minutes)
================================================================================

STEP 1 - Landing Page (30 sec)
  - Show live impact counters (meals rescued, kg diverted, CO2 avoided)
  - Point out the 3 pillars: AI Forecasting, Surplus Marketplace, Verified Impact
  - Show active surplus food cards at the bottom

STEP 2 - Login as Restaurant (15 sec)
  - Go to /login
  - Click "Demo Restaurant" button (auto-fills credentials)
  - Click "Sign In"

STEP 3 - Restaurant Dashboard (30 sec)
  - Show KPI cards: today's forecast, expected buffer, pending requests
  - Point out the weekly demand chart (weekend spikes visible)
  - Show the pending NGO request from Hope Meal Foundation

STEP 4 - AI Demand Forecast (1 min) -- KEY FEATURE
  - Click "Demand Forecast" in sidebar
  - Set target date to 2026-11-08 (Diwali Sunday!)
  - Click "Run Prediction"
  - Show the forecast table: predicted demand, recommended production, surplus
  - Point out the festival detection banner ("Diwali falls on this date")
  - Scroll down to Model Explainability cards
  - Highlight: "Weekend pattern: Sunday orders +22% vs average"
  - Highlight: "Festival multiplier: Diwali boosts dining out"
  - Mention: "High Quality ML Model - 90 records, RandomForestRegressor"

STEP 5 - Approve NGO Request (30 sec)
  - Click "NGO Requests" in sidebar
  - Show the status timeline (Requested -> Approved -> Collected -> Completed)
  - Approve the pending request from Hope Meal Foundation
  - Show that available quantity decreases atomically

STEP 6 - Post Surplus Food (30 sec)
  - Click "Surplus Listings" in sidebar
  - Click "Post New Surplus Food"
  - Fill: dish name, quantity, prep time, expiry, storage temp
  - Point out the food safety checkbox
  - Submit

STEP 7 - Switch to NGO Account (15 sec)
  - Logout
  - Login as NGO (click "Demo NGO" button)

STEP 8 - NGO Dashboard & Marketplace (45 sec)
  - Show pickup alerts for approved requests
  - Click "Browse Surplus Food" to see the marketplace
  - Show search filters (keyword, category, dietary type)
  - Click "Request Servings" on a listing
  - Fill quantity and pickup time, submit

STEP 9 - Complete a Feeding Drive (30 sec)
  - Go to "My Requests"
  - Show the status timeline tracking
  - If any request is in "Collected" status, click "Mark Distribution Complete"
  - Enter beneficiaries served count
  - Show the record appears in "Feeding Drives" history

STEP 10 - Impact Page (15 sec)
  - Click "Impact" in top navigation
  - Show verified metrics from actual database records
  - Show Chart.js pie chart and bar chart
  - Point out: "All metrics calculated from real MongoDB records"


================================================================================
                        KEY TALKING POINTS
================================================================================

  * "FoodCycle tackles the ROOT CAUSE of food waste - inaccurate demand
    prediction - not just the symptoms."

  * "Our ML engine uses RandomForestRegressor with chronological validation,
    so no future data leaks into training. It considers weekday patterns,
    Indian festivals, and recent momentum trends."

  * "When a restaurant has only 5 leftover servings and two NGOs both want
    4, our atomic MongoDB operations prevent overbooking."

  * "Every donated meal is tracked end-to-end: from kitchen prep timestamp
    to NGO distribution, with beneficiary counts logged for transparent
    sustainability reporting."

  * "Food safety is non-negotiable. Listings require temperature disclosures
    (Hot-held >63C or Chilled <5C), allergen declarations, and strict
    pickup deadlines."
