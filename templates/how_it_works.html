How FoodCycle Operates

A seamless, four-step circular pipeline connecting kitchen forecasts to verified community meals.

1

Forecast Daily Kitchen Demand

Restaurants record daily dish sales or load past order history. FoodCycle’s prediction model analyzes day-of-week trends, weekend patterns, and Indian festival spikes (Diwali, Eid, Holi) to generate dish-by-dish production targets with custom safety buffers.

Machine Learning Demand Regression
2

Publish Fresh Surplus with Safety Guidelines

When prepared dishes remain unserved after lunch or dinner service, restaurants publish a listing specifying exact quantity, cooking time, holding temperature (Hot-held >63°C, Chilled <5°C), allergens, and strict pickup deadline.

Temperature & Freshness Verification
3

NGO Claim & Atomic Quantity Reservation

Verified NGOs browse the live marketplace and request specific quantities matching their distribution capacity. FoodCycle locks the requested portions atomically in MongoDB to prevent overbooking, then notifies the restaurant for pickup coordination.

Atomic Database Concurrency Protection
4

Verified Collection & Measured Community Impact

Upon receiving the meals, the NGO completes the handover and registers the exact number of people nourished. Both partners receive certified sustainability data documenting food waste diverted and meals delivered.

Verified Real-World Impact Records
