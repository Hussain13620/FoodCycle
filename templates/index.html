How FoodCycle Solves the Food Waste Crisis

Nearly 30% of commercial kitchen food waste happens before serving due to inaccurate prep estimates. We tackle the root cause first, then salvage whatever surplus remains safely.

1. AI Demand Forecasting

Our scikit-learn regression models analyze weekday rushes, Indian festival dates (Diwali, Eid, Holi), and historical order trends to generate accurate prep guidelines.

2. Surplus Food Marketplace

Inevitably, safe surplus occurs. Restaurants post dishes with prep times, storage temperatures, and strict expiration windows. Local NGOs reserve quantities atomically.

3. Verified Human Impact

No food goes unrecorded. When an NGO completes a distribution, the actual quantity and beneficiaries served are logged to create transparent sustainability reports.
